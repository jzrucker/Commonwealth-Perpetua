# Commonwealth Perpetua
## Legacy & Institutional Investor Portfolio Intelligence

---

The institutions that fund the alternative investment ecosystem — university endowments, pension trusts, hospital retirement systems, union benefit funds — collectively deploy trillions of dollars into private equity and alternative investments on behalf of the people and missions they serve.

Their investment offices are among the most consequential in the world. And the complexity they manage has never been greater.

**The gap is structural.** A major pension fund. Fifteen investment staff. Hundreds of fund relationships. Thousands of underlying companies generating returns across dozens of geographies and asset classes. They receive quarterly statements, capital notices, annual filings, and curated presentations. Almost none of it arrives as a coherent picture of what their capital is actually doing.

Commonwealth Perpetua closes that gap.

---

## What This Is

An AI-native portfolio intelligence platform built specifically for institutional investors — turning the documents they already receive into the independent analytical view they've never had.

**Portfolio Intelligence** — Map every underlying company your capital is invested in. See where returns actually came from, company by company. Surface concentration risk you didn't know you had.

**Operational Intelligence** — Synthesize capital calls, distribution notices, fund communications, and legal agreements into a unified signal stream. Understand what is happening across your portfolio in real time.

**Financial Intelligence** — Independent valuation analysis, tax efficiency identification, fee impact modeling. Your office gets a number on a quarterly statement. We give you an independent view of whether that number makes sense.

**Internal Efficiency** — Record-keeping, administrative infrastructure, and the analytical foundation to steward your capital with the rigor your beneficiaries deserve.

## Why This Exists

The capital flows from the LP. The information flows away from the LP. That asymmetry has defined institutional investing for decades — because no one has built the infrastructure to close it from the LP side of the table.

The missions these institutions serve — retirement security, education, healthcare, workers — depend on investment performance. They deserve infrastructure that matches the scale and importance of what they manage.
